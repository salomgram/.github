### Why Salomgram

The most common way people in Uzbekistan lose money from their cards is a fake app sent in a chat:
`Rasmlar.apk`, `Posilka.apk`, `To'lov.apk`. Once installed, it reads SMS codes and empties the card.

Salomgram checks every Android app file on the device, offline, before it can be opened:

- **ScamGuard** reads the file's manifest and flags SMS access, notification reading,
  accessibility control and device-admin requests — the permissions bank stealers need.
- **Disguised files** such as `photo.jpg.apk` are caught by name and by content.
- **One tap** deletes the file, blocks the sender and reports spam.

### Built with care

- Built on the open-source [Telegram for Android](https://github.com/DrKLO/Telegram) codebase and the official Telegram API.
- Uzbek-first interface and its own visual identity.
- Designed to follow the Telegram API Terms of Service and Google Play policies.
